# Blog

The content module for Pubvana. Write posts, organize them with categories and tags, and publish them to your site.

## Features

- Posts with categories and tags
- Revision history, so you can review and restore earlier versions
- Preview links for drafts, so you can review before publishing
- RSS and Atom feeds
- Search across posts
- Blocks for recent posts, categories, tags, archive, and related posts
- Comments on posts

## Usage

Write and manage posts under **Content → Blog**. Categories and tags are managed there too.

Posts appear on your site at `/blog` (or your configured prefix). Feeds are at `/feed`, `/rss`, and `/atom.xml`.
