# Pages

Static pages for Pubvana. Create About, Contact, Terms, and similar pages with SEO metadata, draft or published status, revision history, and optional comments.

## Features

- Static pages with SEO metadata
- Draft and published status
- Draft preview links you can share with anyone
- Revision history with restore
- Optional comments
- Soft delete
- Searchable and linkable in navigation
- Can be offered as the site homepage

## Usage

Open **Content → Pages** to manage your pages. Published pages appear at `/page/@slug` on your site.

A draft page shows a **Preview** button in the list and on the edit page. Click it to open the page in your theme. Anyone can open the link, and publishing replaces it.

Managing pages requires the pages.manage permission.
